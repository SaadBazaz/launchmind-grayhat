Your personalized assistant for secure, transparent, and convenient collaboration across devices at any time; a reliable, versatile tool that ensures your workflow remains simple and efficient.

Melon Features

  • Voice Conversion Functionality

    Users interact with the application using voice commands; the moment they speak their thoughts, it is translated into text within seconds.

  • AI-Assisted Responses

    The 'Assistant' feature enables users to get quick responses to direct and indirectly prompted requests without leaving Melon. The database of AI-approved information makes it easy for the user, and no extra input is required.

  • Backup on Cloud

    Voice notes are automatically backed up in real-time to the cloud; there's a high likelihood that your Melon experience will be secure and easily accessible at any time.

  • Cloud Sync, Real-Time Updates

    Melon ensures all data is updated in real-time across all devices connected for quick access even when you move or leave, ensuring no need for extra information.
